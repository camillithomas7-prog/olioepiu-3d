Our story

Since the summer of 2010, Olio e Più has brought the essence of Naples to the heart of Greenwich Village. A modern trattoria where Old World recipes meet New York energy, and where every dinner feels like a festive meal with family and friends.

2010The summer we opened our doors in the Village
74Seats, plus an open patio in bloom
7Days a week, from lunch to midnight
1Wood brick oven for our hand-stretched pizza
